# PURSUE OS — Licensing

> **Source:** PURSUE OS master handoff (authoritative). Restored 2026-08-09.
> **Category:** A — Authoritative licensing decisions.

## Locked Decisions

- PURSUE's own code is licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**.
- **Third-party software distributed with PURSUE may carry separate licenses** — do not incorrectly claim that all third-party Linux packages are Apache-2.0.
- Keep third-party licensing separate from PURSUE's Apache-2.0 license.
- LICENSE is the full Apache-2.0 text; do not shorten it.
- NOTICE is deliberately kept short and project-specific.

## Governing Files

- `LICENSE` — full Apache-2.0 text.
- `NOTICE` — short project-specific notice.
- `TRADEMARKS.md` — trademark policy (branding protection separate from license).
- `docs/legal/PROJECT_LICENSE.md` — rationale for Apache-2.0.
- `docs/legal/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSE_POLICY.md` — handling third-party components.
- `docs/legal/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` — registry of third-party components in use.
