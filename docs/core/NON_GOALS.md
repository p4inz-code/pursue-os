# PURSUE OS — Non-Goals

> **Source:** PURSUE OS master handoff (authoritative). Restored 2026-08-09.
> **Category:** A — Authoritative project decisions.

These are explicit non-goals. Do not add them to V1, and do not allow them to become the direction of the project.

## Identity Non-Goals

- **Not another Kali Linux clone.**
- **Not a cyberpunk/hacker OS.**
- **Not a generic Linux distribution.**
- **Not a giant collection of random tools.**
- No restarting naming or product planning.
- No reconsideration of Apache-2.0 unless a real legal requirement appears.

## Claims Non-Goals

- No claim of a stable release before one exists.
- No fake ISO or ISO links.
- No anonymity/security guarantees — especially never claiming Tor provides complete anonymity.
- No fake screenshots, release badges, download buttons, benchmark claims, stability claims, user counts, or feature-completion claims until they actually exist.

## Engineering Non-Goals

- No redesign of the entire repository.
- No dozens of unnecessary folders.
- No random UI mockups before the core exists.
- No installation of every OSINT tool immediately (curated defaults instead).
- No building every plugin before the core.
- No overengineering of V1.
- No assuming unverified technical facts — research when a decision benefits from current information.

## AI Non-Goals

- No autonomous AI.
- No AI altering evidence or silently modifying sources.
- No external AI APIs in V1.
- No AI as the source of truth.
