# PURSUE OS — Glossary

> **Source:** PURSUE OS master handoff (authoritative). Restored 2026-08-09.
> **Category:** A — Authoritative project decisions.

Terms are defined as used by the master handoff.

- **OSINT** — Open-Source Intelligence: investigation based on publicly available sources.
- **SOCMINT** — Social Media Intelligence: investigation of social media platforms and activity.
- **GEOINT** — Geospatial Intelligence: investigation involving geographic/location information.
- **CTI** — Cyber Threat Intelligence: investigation of threats, actors, and indicators.
- **DFIR** — Digital Forensics and Incident Response.
- **Infrastructure intelligence** — investigation of technical infrastructure (domains, hosting, certificates, etc.).
- **Media analysis** — analysis of images, video, audio, and documents as evidence.
- **Investigation Terminal** — flagship purpose-built command-line environment for investigators.
- **Investigation Browser** — flagship secure, configurable research browser with integrated Tor capabilities.
- **Flagship** — a primary, differentiating interface of PURSUE.
- **Evidence** — collected information that is the immutable source of truth for a case.
- **Provenance** — the recorded origin and handling history of evidence and sources.
- **Case** — an organized investigation container holding evidence, sources, notes, relationships, timelines, findings, reports, and exports.
- **Source** — the origin of information; provenance must connect information to its source.
- **Investigator notes** — investigator-controlled metadata attached to a case.
- **Investigation graph** — the connected model of entities and relationships in an investigation.
- **Timeline** — chronological organization of investigation events.
- **Onion / .onion** — Tor hidden-service addressing used for onion search capability.
- **S-class security** — the handoff's term for PURSUE's strong default security baseline.
- **AI assistant** — optional local AI that summarizes/explains/organizes only; never the source of truth.
- **Non-AI mode** — PURSUE operating without AI assistance enabled.
- **Plugin** — an extension unit of the modular PURSUE platform (V1 foundation, V2 stronger system).
