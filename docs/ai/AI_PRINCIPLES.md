# PURSUE OS — AI Principles

> **Source:** PURSUE OS master handoff (authoritative). Restored 2026-08-09.
> **Category:** A — Authoritative AI principles.

1. **Assistant only.** AI helps; the investigator decides. AI never becomes the source of truth.
2. **Evidence is untouchable.** AI never manipulates evidence, silently modifies sources, or fabricates investigation facts.
3. **Consent.** AI never automates investigator judgment without consent.
4. **Optional by default.** AI mode has an on/off; users are asked at startup; pros and cons are explained clearly; users are never scared or forced; AI can be disabled in settings.
5. **Local-first in V1.** Local models only; external online AI APIs are not supported in V1; users may download one of several supported models.
6. **Choice of models.** Approximately three categories: fast, deep-thinking, coding/technical — with careful handling of higher-assistance options within security, legal, and project boundaries.
7. **Non-AI mode exists.** The system is fully usable without AI.
8. **Transparent assistance.** AI's contributions (summaries, explanations, recommendations) are optional and investigator-controlled; the source remains the source of truth.
